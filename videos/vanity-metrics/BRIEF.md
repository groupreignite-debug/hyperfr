# Brief: Vanity Metrics (شو يعني؟ #2)

Teach one idea simply: **likes, views and followers feel like progress, but the numbers that matter are the
ones that turn into customers.** Teach, don't sell. The viewer should finish thinking
"صح، أنا بتطلّع عالأرقام الغلط." It sets up the "Comment AUDIT" offers later in the month.

## Must-haves (from the client brief)
- Same visual system as the Incrementality reel: charcoal `#1A1A1A` (dominant), orange `#FF4D1A` (meaning),
  off-white `#F5F1EA` (text), gold `#FFB000` at most twice (not used here). No other colours.
- Alexandria (Arabic + Latin, 300–900). Numbers 3–5× their labels. Correct RTL shaping. English terms in caps Latin.
- Same 72px grid with slow drift; soft masked rises, counters, dot state changes, line draws (`power3.out` / `expo.out`).
- One world: the dots carry the story 1,000 → 10 → 2.
- Arabic subtitles burned in = the spoken line, one at a time, bottom safe area (above the bottom 300px, clear of
  the right-edge icons). Only `Vanity Metrics`, `Likes`, `Views`, `Followers`, `Reach`, `Actionable Metrics`
  are in Latin, in orange.
- The official logo (`assets/reignite/reignite-group-logo.png`, copied unchanged from the Incrementality build)
  with only fade/scale. End card: logo only, no text.
- **Honesty rule:** every number is a hypothetical example framed by "تخيّل". No real statistics, clients or results.
