# Brief: Incrementality, هل الإعلان فعلاً جابلك مبيعات إضافية؟

Premium Arabic educational motion-graphics reel for Reignite Group. It explains the difference
between **Attribution** and **Incrementality** to a non-specialist viewer. The aim is to teach, not to sell.
The viewer should finish thinking "ما كنت بعرف هالمعلومة."
