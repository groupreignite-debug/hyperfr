# STORYBOARD: Incrementality

## Video direction
Dark editorial information design. A charcoal canvas with a barely visible 72px technical grid that drifts slowly
(parallax). Alexandria (Arabic + Latin) runs from 300 to 900 weight. Numbers are 3–5× the size of their labels.
Orange marks meaning and gold is used twice at most. Motion is soft: masked rises, counters, dot-state changes and
line draws, with `power3.out` / `expo.out` eases. The camera stays still apart from a slow push-in on the key
moments. One world: the customer dots carry the story from 100 → 40 → 25 → 15, the word INCREMENTALITY shrinks
into the split-screen header, and the control-group dots form unit-bar charts.
